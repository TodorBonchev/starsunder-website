About the Game

Starsunder is a 2D top-down multiplayer spaceship arena game built in Unity 6 with Quantum. Battle 2–8 players in an inertia-based arena, master unique ship archetypes, and unleash special abilities to dominate the match. Initially available on mobile, starting with Google Play, with Apple App Store support coming soon.
